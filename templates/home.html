Clinical Workflow

How It Works

A seamless 3-step decision support workflow designed for ophthalmologists, clinicians, and academic evaluators.

Step 01

Upload Retinal Image

Upload a digital color fundus photograph (OD/OS) in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format with instant pre-validation and client-side preview.

Step 02

Deep CNN Analysis

The convolutional neural network extracts microscopic lesion features, detecting microaneurysms, hemorrhages, and abnormal capillary proliferation.

Step 03

View Prediction & Metrics

Receive instant severity categorization (Grade 0–4), confidence probability scores, class distribution graphs, and clinical triage recommendations.
